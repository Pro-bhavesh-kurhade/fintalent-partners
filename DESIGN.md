# FinTalent Partners Design System — Neo-Editorial Fintech Studio (v2.0)

## Design Philosophy
1. **Zero Turnaround Over Scroll Fatigue**: Both Candidates and Recruiters reach live actionable inventory (`Open Roles` & `Pre-Vetted Talent`) immediately below the hero fold, with 1-click pre-fill straight into a single-screen Express Action Studio.
2. **Funky Neo-Editorial Contrast**: Pairs tactile architectural & abstract 3D studio art (`/images/*`) with punchy Electric Acid Lime (`#C8FF32`), Signal Coral (`#FF6B4A`), Cobalt (`#3B5BFF`), and Emerald Mint (`#12B886`) against either **Obsidian Acid Studio (`#090B10`)** or crisp neo-brutalist **Warm Studio Cream (`#FAF7F0` + `#0D1017` ink shadows)**.

## Typography
- **Display & Headings**: `Bricolage Grotesque` (700–800 weight, `-0.03em` tracking)
- **UI & Prose**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400–800 weight)
- **Data, Compensation, Codes & Badges**: `JetBrains Mono` (500–700 weight)

## Core Tokens
- `--accent-lime`: `#C8FF32` — Primary high-velocity candidate & CTA highlight
- `--accent-coral`: `#FF6B4A` — Recruiter hiring desk & urgent mandate badge
- `--accent-cobalt`: `#3B5BFF` — Practice tags & secondary interactive controls
- `--accent-mint`: `#12B886` — ICAI verification & live pipeline active signals
- Dark Canvas: `#090B10` with `#111522` elevated glass cards
- Light Canvas: `#FAF7F0` with `#FFFFFF` cards, `1.5px solid #0D1017` borders, and `5px 5px 0px #0D1017` tactile pop shadows
